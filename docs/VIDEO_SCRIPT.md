# ScorvoAI — Video Script & Narration

**Duration:** 3:00 (hard cap)
**Word count:** ~470 spoken words (≈150 wpm)
**Tone:** Cinematic, hopeful, grounded. Not salesy. Like a Netflix documentary trailer that becomes a product film.
**Music:** Sparse piano + soft strings in Acts 1-3 → swells in Act 4 → falls back for the close.

---

## Cold open · 0:00 – 0:25

**Visual:**
- Slow drone pan over a small town in rural India at dawn. Fog. A single light on in one window.
- Cut: a young woman, late teens, walking a dusty road with a heavy textbook bag.
- Cut: tight shot of a hand turning a page in a torn government-exam guidebook by candlelight.
- Cut: wide shot of a packed Delhi coaching center — hundreds of heads, one teacher at a whiteboard.

**Narration:**
> Every year, **thirty million Indians** wake up before sunrise — and study for an exam that could change everything.

*(beat — natural sound of a page turning)*

> Most of them only teach in three cities.
> And the current affairs that decide half your exam — changed yesterday.

**On-screen text (small, bottom-left):**
`Sources: NCERT, Annual Status of Education Report 2024`

## Act 2 · The Product in Action · 0:55 – 1:55

**Visual:**
Screen recordings, full-bleed phone mockups, smooth tab transitions. Each segment ~12-15 seconds.

### 2a. Lesson of the Day (0:55 – 1:10)
- Show Home → "Lesson of the Day" card → tap → markdown lesson streams in.

> It starts with you.
> A **3-minute lesson** — generated this morning, on the exact chapter where you scored lowest yesterday.

### 2b. Real Current Affairs (1:10 – 1:25)
- Scroll to "Top News Today" → tap a card → external article opens with source URL highlighted.

> It tells you what just happened.
> Top news, **fact-checked from real sources**, with the exam angle explained — never made up by an AI.

### 2c. Smart Quiz (1:25 – 1:40)
- Quiz tab → tap "Smart Practice" → quiz streams in question by question → answer one → result with explanation.

> It pushes you.
> **Smart Practice** picks five questions on your weakest chapters, at exactly the difficulty you can handle.

### 2d. Camera Solver (1:40 – 1:55)
- Solver screen → tap camera → frame a printed math problem → text auto-populates → tap Solve → step-by-step solution streams in.

> Stuck on a problem?
> Point your camera at it. ScorvoAI walks you through the solution, step by step.

## Voiceover Direction

- **Voice:** Calm, sincere, mid-30s. Indian English accent preferred (authenticity matters for this story). Not corporate, not overly emotional.
- **Pacing:** Slow in cold open (let visuals breathe), pick up tempo through Act 2, slow again for Act 4.
- **Pauses:** Marked with *(beat)* — typically 0.5-1 s of silence with music carrying.
- **Emphasis:** Bold words in the script are the key beats. Don't push them too hard; let the writing do the work.

## What to record live (in-app)

- 60-second screen capture of: Open app → Home → Daily Lesson tap → back → News tap → external link opens → Quiz tab → Smart Practice → answer 1 question → Solver tap → camera OCR → solution stream.
- Capture at 60 fps, 1080×1920, then speed-ramp the non-critical transitions in post.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't show competitors / criticize them.
- ❌ Don't use stock footage of "AI brains" or futuristic UI. The product is the hero.
- ❌ Don't use AI-generated narration — use a real human voice. Authenticity is the whole point.
- ❌ Don't promise specific outcomes ("crack UPSC in 6 months"). Stay grounded.
